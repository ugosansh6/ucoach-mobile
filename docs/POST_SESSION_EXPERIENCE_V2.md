# POST-SESSION EXPERIENCE V2

## Goal
Turn the post-session screen into a premium coaching close-out moment rather than a generic stack of cards.

## Product principles
- Celebrate the completed session immediately.
- Separate what this session produced from the athlete's broader trajectory.
- Never invent progress when the evidence does not support it.
- Show what UGEROD retained and what that changes for the next coaching decisions.
- Keep social sharing secondary, but make the preview editorial and event-driven.
- Do not block the whole screen while secondary analysis loads.

## V2 hierarchy
1. Hero: `Bien joué.` + duration/environment/result.
2. `Ce qui compte aujourd'hui`: session-scoped evidence only.
3. `Le Coach`: `Ce que je retiens` + `Pour la suite`.
4. Conditional skill question when useful.
5. `Partager ce moment`: adaptive story preview.
6. Finish / progression navigation.

## Guardrails
- A global 4-week progression signal must not be presented as if it was caused by this session.
- Environment, fatigue or pain adaptations must not automatically reduce the athlete's capability.
- Missing metrics remain missing; they are not converted into synthetic records.
- The native share action still sends text in this design-validation pass. Image export is intentionally deferred until the visual direction is approved.
